
There are generic requirements for solution architects, and on top of this there are practice-specific
requirements: for Intelligent Enterprise Practice (for example, DW/BI/BigData/DevOps), for Advanced
Technology Practice (for example, Java/.Net/Integration/Agile), and for Digital Engagement Practice (for example, Hybris/Sitecore/Mobile/Front-end).

#### Level 1-2

- Small-to-medium size solutions
- Participates in pre-sales
- 1+ platforms
- 2+ domains
- SA theory and practice

#### Level 3

- Large-size solutions
- Leads pre-sales
- 2+ platforms
- 3+ domains
- Enterprise architecture theory
- Technology roadmap, blueprints

#### Level 4-5

- Large-size solutions with EA specifics
- Leads "world-class" pre-sales
- 3+ platforms
- 4+ domains
- Wide enterprise architecture practice
- Can be CTO
