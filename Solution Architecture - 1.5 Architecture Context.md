There is no denying that the technical environment certainly influences an architect. The number of technologies is constantly growing. There are a lot of frameworks, tools, platforms and
new technologies appearing every day. We see what is happening on the market, and when making a
decision regarding a tool/platform for our project, we are often influenced by trends and buzzwords.

## Architect's influence

- Positive Experience with Technologies and Patterns
- Bad Experiences are Avoided in New Designs
- Influence of Trends
- Influence of Education and Training

And an architect can be influenced by his/her own experience.
All of us have previous experience; we’ve tried several styles, patterns, platforms. We’ve tried different
architectural approaches and tactics. We have both good and bad experiences. We can be influenced
by trends and so on. It happens that we try to apply all our knowledge and experience to the current
system. This is a very important type of influence, and it's clear with every new project, our experience
grows. This ends up having an influence on our decision-making.


![[Pasted image 20250413223339.png]]

It’s very important to understand that this is not one-way flow but a cycle. Because after we create the
system, it influences stakeholders, organizational environment, technical environment and architect’s
experience.

**How can a system influence stakeholders?**
The architecture can affect all the requirements for the next system by giving the customer the
opportunity to receive the system in a more reliable, timely and economical manner; to get feedback
from the end-users and so on.
**How can architecture influence an organization’s environment?**
The architecture affects the structure of the developing organization. An architecture prescribes a
structure for a system; as we will see, it specifically prescribes the units of software that must be
implemented (or otherwise obtained) and integrated to form the system. And it often happens that a
software unit or software module matches 1-to-1 to the organization’s unit.
And definitely, the process of system building will affect the **architect’s experience.**
With every project, we gain more and more experience, success stories, and failures, and we constantly
grow as experts.
