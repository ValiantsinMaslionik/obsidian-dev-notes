## Stakeholder Identification

**Techiques**

- **Brainstorming**: Used to produce the stakeholder list and identify stakeholder roles and responsibilities.
- **Interviews**: Used to interact with specific stakeholders to gain more information or knowledge about stakeholder groups.
- **Workshops**: Used to interact with groups of stakeholders to gain more information about stakeholder groups.
- **Mind Mapping**: Used to identify potential stakeholders and help understand the relationships between them.
- **Organizational Modelling**: Used to determine whether the organizational units or people listed have any unique needs and interests that should be considered. Organizational models describe roles and functions in the organization and the ways in which stakeholders interact – which can help to identify stakeholders who will be affected by a change.
- **Process Modelling**: Used to categorize stakeholders by the systems that support their business processes.
- **Stakeholder List, Map, or Personas**: Used to depict the relationship of stakeholders to the solution and to one another.
- **Survey or Questionnaire**: Used to identify shared characteristics of a stakeholder group.

#### Relationships

It's important to remember that each stakeholder has their own view of a solution. At the same time, they are a member of one or more groups (division, friends, communities, etc.). Therefore, their concerns and requirements are heavily impacted by information coming from those groups.

## Stakeholder management

**Tips**

- Identify powerful stakeholders
- Observe stakeholders and their formal/informal relations
- Use the following techniques to analyze relationships
	- Power/Interest matrix
	- Personal interest diagram
	- Social diagram

#### Stakeholder alignment

- **Accountability should be agreed/defined based on activities.** One activity must be associated with a single accountable person only.
- **Responsibility should be defined also based on activities.** More that one responsible person can be associated with the same activity. Same person can be accountable and responsible at the same time.
- **Use RACI matrix to document agreements.**

#### RACI matrix

The RACI matrix outlines the main activities and level of involvement of each role.

> There must be **one accountable** only and **at least one responsible** for an activity in the RACI matrix.
