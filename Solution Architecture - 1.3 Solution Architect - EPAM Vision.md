
#### Technical knowledge

A solution architect must be a technologist by heart, must live and breathe technology.

#### Business Domain Knowledge

A SA should have a wide vision. A SA should be able to work in different technology and business domains. Being an expert in 2-3 business domains and 2-3 different technology platforms is the goal of every solution architect.

#### Methodologies, Process and Practices

A SA should have good design skill (conceptual visualization in mind, vision and understanding of all pieces).

#### Leadership, Communication and Influence

A SA must have good communication, negotiation and presentation skills, as it is their primary responsibility to talk to business, including C-level managers. They identify and document business goals to be archived in the future.
