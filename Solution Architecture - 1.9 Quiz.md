
## Lesson 1

#### Here’s an analysis of your statements about architecture

- Architecture should describe internal details of every element **FALSE**  
Architecture focuses on the high-level design and structure of systems. It does not delve into the internal implementation details of every element—those are usually covered in design specifications.
    
- Architecture elements have attributes **TRUE**  
Architecture elements have attributes, such as functionality, performance, scalability, security, and interfaces, which describe their characteristics and constraints.
    
- Functional requirements are the main driver for architecture **FALSE**  
 While functional requirements are important, architecture is largely driven by **non-functional requirements** such as scalability, reliability, security, and performance. These shape the structure and behavior of the system.
    
- Architecture should describe interfaces of its elements **TRUE** 
Architecture typically includes the definition of interfaces between elements to ensure proper interaction and integration within the system.

#### The correct definition of Solution Architecture is

**Primarily describes a solution details and aims to address the client's business needs**.
Solution architecture focuses on designing and delivering a specific solution that solves a business problem, aligning technical systems and processes to meet client requirements effectively. It often incorporates elements such as system interactions, interfaces, scalability, and integration, but its primary purpose is ensuring the solution meets business objectives. #### The architecture layer that is typically scoped by a project or a concrete team and focuses on low-level architecture descriptions is

**Technical Architecture**.
Technical Architecture deals with the detailed, technology-specific aspects of a solution, including frameworks, platforms, programming languages, APIs, and infrastructure components. It's closely tied to implementation and operational aspects, making it suitable for projects or teams that need to work on specific systems or applications.
